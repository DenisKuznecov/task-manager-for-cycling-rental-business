B Balanced landscape hierarchy

Two equal queues, one glance

My Work and Available Now receive equal-width panels so a mounted landscape tablet shows current responsibility and next capacity simultaneously. Repeated card geometry makes facts comparable, while clear section boundaries prevent the two work states from blending.

Portrait adaptation Stack the equal panels with My Work first; retain identical card anatomy. The Action Item ribbon becomes a compact tile below both sections with two side-by-side controls.

C Phase-scannable hierarchy

Operational rows for rapid next-task selection

Dense but touch-safe rows align phase, dates, ownership, and action into stable columns, making differences visible before opening a task. My Work and Available Now remain separate sections; colored phase rails are supplemental to explicit phase labels and large action buttons.

Portrait adaptation Each row becomes a stacked card: bike/order first, then phase + dates + assignee, then a full-width action. Preserve section order and phase labels; never reduce to a color-only rail.
