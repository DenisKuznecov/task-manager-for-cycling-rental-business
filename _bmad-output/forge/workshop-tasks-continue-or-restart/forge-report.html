Forge Report — Workshop Tasks

Session: 2026-08-18  ·  Owner: Den  ·  Question: kill it, restart it, or continue?

Verdict: continue — pivoted. Neither of the two options Den walked in with survived. The plan was over-built for the shop he has and under-built for the business he wants; the shipped parts are real value and stay.

What was on the anvil

A "workshop tasks" feature (digital bike-prep and return checklists) whose plan had grown to 9 epics and ~50 stories. Den was 12 stories in, buried in Epic 2's canonical-ingestion vocabulary, three sprint-change proposals deep in four days, and could no longer tell what the AI was building or why it kept asking for clarification.

Locked decisions

  • Keep as shipped: Epic 1 (checklist template library) and stories 2.1–2.3 (webhook security, Next 16 / Node 24 upgrade, toolchain pins).
  • Keep frozen: the canonical layer 2.4–2.9 — no extensions, deferred items stay deferred; one slim wiring story (webhook → canonical fetch-and-apply + refetch-on-claim) replaces stories 2.11 and 2.12 and finally plugs the dark engine in. Story 2.10 continues in its separate agent window.
  • Killed: 2.13 (revoke legacy writers) and 2.14 (rollout control plane) — exactness insurance for a failure whose full cost is a walk back to the bike rack.
  • Compressed: epics 3–9 shrink from 34 stories to ~13. The M2 second-mechanic re-check survives on hard evidence (the paper checklist really has two columns and dual signatures); claim races demote to a unique constraint; Epic 9 becomes simply using the thing.
  • Franchise ambition: direction only, nothing signed — no tenancy features; the pitch will be a system Den's own shop demonstrably runs on. Shop-scope columns were offered and consciously rejected.

Weak points that survived scrutiny

  • If a franchisee ever signs, workshop history will need a tenancy retrofit migration — a known, accepted debt.
  • The frozen canonical layer carries known inert machinery (fingerprint columns, replacement-chain fields) that will confuse future readers until something consumes or removes it.
  • An unexecuted forward-revert action item for Story 2.7 still sits open in sprint-status.yaml.
  • Whether Booqable bumps order.updated_at on nested-only changes remains unverified; the comparator could quarantine legitimate additions (deferred, watch in practice).
