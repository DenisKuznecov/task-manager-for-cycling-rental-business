- (direction by user) Replace the /customers synchronization-status table with a customer directory.
- (capability by user) CAP-1: Staff can paginate and search the customer directory by name, email, or phone, and see Name, Email, Phone, Birthday, and last order date.
- (capability by user) CAP-2: Staff can open a customer details drawer containing table data, full address, Google/Holded/Mailchimp upload statuses, all customer orders, and all associated partners from partner-promo orders.
- (capability by user) CAP-3: Each customer order in the drawer is actionable and takes staff to that order.
- (constraint by user) The directory table must show a skeleton while search or pagination reloads its data.
- (constraint) Use authenticated, RLS-respecting reads and keep aggregation, joins, search, pagination, last-order derivation, and partner rollup in PostgreSQL views or RPCs rather than client-side arrays.
- (assumption) Customer cards means a customer details drawer opened from a directory row, matching the requested Orders drawer precedent.
- (question) Which date should last order date represent: order creation, rental start, or rental stop?
- (question) Does order navigation mean the existing /orders?order=<id> details drawer, or a new standalone order route?
- (question) Does a partner count only when both orders.partner_id and orders.partner_promo are populated, and should the drawer show the partner name alone or its promo code too?
